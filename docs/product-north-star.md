# Product North Star

The Product North Star defines the vision and guiding principles for Nutrimap. It ensures that all development efforts align with the app's core purpose and long-term goals.

## Vision

Nutrimap empowers individuals to take control of their wellness journey by providing tools to track and improve their health goals. The app focuses on simplicity, user-friendliness, and careful handling of personal data.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Wellness Focused:** Prioritize features that support users' wellness goals, such as hair health, hormonal balance, immunity, and nervous system support.
2. **Simplicity:** Keep the app intuitive and easy to use.
3. **Data Responsibility:** Handle personal data with care and transparency.
4. **Iterative Development:** Start small, validate ideas, and expand based on user feedback.

## Why This Matters

Defining the Product North Star ensures that every decision made during development aligns with the app's purpose and values. It helps avoid feature creep and keeps the team focused on delivering value to users.
